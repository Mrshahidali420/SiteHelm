# Product Identity Brief

## Audience

WordPress agencies operating heterogeneous client sites built with combinations of WordPress core, Elementor, ACF, Meta Box, and future supported plugins.

## Category

A secure WordPress MCP operations platform delivered as one plugin.

## Promise

One plugin for operating WordPress and supported plugins through MCP, with consistent discovery, permission, preview, verification, audit, and recovery behavior.

## Differentiators

1. Internally modular integrations bundled into one customer-facing plugin.
2. Safe writes with strict validation, two-step plans, state verification, and recoverable history where supported.
3. Guided connection, compatibility diagnostics, and a controlled active tool surface.
4. Agency-oriented permission profiles, evidence, workflows, and future multi-site operations.

## Tone

Technically credible, direct, calm, and operational. The product never describes itself as magical, autonomous, error-proof, or risk-free. Claims distinguish tested behavior from roadmap intent.

## Naming Constraints

- Do not use `WordPress`, `Elementor`, `EMCP`, another plugin trademark, or a confusing variation of a competitor.
- Do not use `WP` in a way that implies an official WordPress affiliation.
- Prefer a name that remains credible when the product expands beyond Elementor.
- Prefer simple English pronunciation and spelling.
- A `.com` observation is useful but does not determine the product name.
- Search results, repository results, plugin-directory results, and RDAP records are preliminary collision checks, not legal trademark clearance.
- No domain, account, repository, or plugin slug is reserved during Phase 1.

### Recommendation

**SiteHelm** is recommended because it communicates controlled site operations immediately, sounds credible in an agency proposal, and does not tie the product to Elementor or one AI client. **ControlLoom** is the strongest lower-collision alternative if distinctiveness and possible `.com` availability outweigh immediate category clarity.

## Selected Identity

**SiteHelm** was selected by the user on 2026-07-24 from the three screened finalists.

- Product name: SiteHelm, pronounced "site helm".
- Category statement: a secure WordPress MCP operations platform delivered as one plugin.
- Value proposition: one plugin for operating WordPress and supported plugins through MCP, with consistent discovery, permission, preview, verification, audit, and recovery behavior.
- ControlLoom and StackRelay are unselected candidates with no reserved or pending status.
- The registered state of `sitehelm.com` is a recorded observation, not a blocker; naming does not depend on that domain.
- No domain, social account, repository, or plugin slug is reserved. Any public reservation or announcement requires separate explicit user approval.
